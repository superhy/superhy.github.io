Research focus

AI that helps explain disease, not just predict it.

Computational pathology

Multi-scale histomorphology modelling for molecular trait prediction, cancer relapse analysis, fibrosis phenotyping, and foundation-model interpretation.

Interpretable foundation models

Behavioural analysis, adaptive fusion, and efficient feature perception methods that reveal when medical AI is robust, fragile, or biologically meaningful.

Multimodal biomedical AI

Cross-modal links between histology, spatial transcriptomics, endoscopy, clinical records, and dynamic tumour microenvironment evolution.
